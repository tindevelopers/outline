Switcher concept

In-app workspace switcher

Lives at the top of the Outline sidebar on every tenant subdomain. The list is the same membership-filtered set the Launchpad selector shows, fetched from the authenticated session. Switching hands off with a transfer token, so no logout and no re-auth.

Your workspaces

Patterns borrowed, adapted to TIN

  • Slack: workspace identity pinned at the top of the sidebar with a menu. We keep the position, drop the channel-style density.
  • Linear and Notion: the switcher lists only workspaces you belong to, each with its own mark. Ours adds the tenant URL in mono so clients can verify where they are.
  • Atlassian: a clear "current" row with a check, so the menu never feels like a logout screen.
  • Keyboard path: the fork already ships a "Switch workspace" command bar action. It becomes a child of this same menu data, reachable with Cmd K.
  • TIN rule: switching never logs you out of the workspace you left. Each tenant keeps its own host-scoped session; the handoff mints a transfer token for the target tenant only.
  • TIN rule: the menu and the Launchpad selector read from one source, the authenticated membership list. Nothing is cached publicly or pre-auth.
